A deadpan weather forecast for a city that does not exist, for make.fun's "Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist" bounty. `main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

The entry:

> Good evening from Inversia, where rain falls upwards at 12 millimetres an hour; the weather office filed it under paperwork and told residents not to look up.

In Inversia the rain falls upwards, and the weather office treats it as an admin matter: filed under paperwork, residents told not to look up. Absurd, measured, and delivered like a real broadcast.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
